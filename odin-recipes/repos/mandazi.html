Mandazi Recipe

Ingredients

  1. 3 cups of all Purpose flour
  2. 2 tsp instant yeast
  3. 5 tbsp sugar
  4. 1 tsp baking powder
  5. 1/2 tsp ground cardamom
  6. 1/2 tsp salt
  7. 1 cup luke warm milk
  8. 4 tbsp melted butter/margarine
  9. 1 whole egg

Method

  1. Mix the dry ingredients in a bowl and set aside
  2. Add the wet ingredients into a separate bowl and mix well.
  3. Now add the wet ingredients to the dry ingredients and knead into a dough.
  4. Cover with a damp cloth and set aside to rise for 1 hour or until the dough has doubled in size.
  5. Roll out the dough (1cm in height) on a lightly floured surface. Cut out shapes. You could cut them into small squares or diamond shapes. You can even use a glass to cut out round shapes.
  6. In a thick bottomed pan, add enough oil to deep fry. The oil is ready to use when you put a wooden skewer in it and bubbles form around the skewer.
  7. Drop in the cut dough shapes and lower the heat to avoid the outside burning while the inside is uncooked. Flip the mandazi over every 30 sec and cook for about 2 min.
  8. Enjoy!
